    <div class="eyebrow">Privacy</div>
    <h1 class="patch-title">What AUXSAYS records about your visit</h1>
    <p class="patch-subtitle">AUXSAYS has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email address. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see which pages are useful.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="panel methodology-grid reveal-up reveal-delay-1">
    <article class="methodology-card methodology-card--primary">
      <h2>Google Analytics</h2>
      <p>When you open a page on auxsays.com, your browser loads Google Analytics 4. It sends Google which page you are on, the page that linked you here, when and for how long you visit, and basic facts about your browser, device and screen. Google uses the IP address your browser connects from to estimate an approximate location, such as a country or city.</p>
      <p>Google Analytics stores cookies in your browser (named <code>_ga</code>, and <code>_ga_</code> followed by an identifier) so that repeat visits from the same browser are counted together.</p>
      <p>AUXSAYS looks at these reports to see which pages people use and which need work. AUXSAYS does not sell this data.</p>
      <p>Google Analytics runs only on auxsays.com. It does not run on copies of the site built or served anywhere else, and it does not run in browsers that identify themselves as automated.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="methodology-card">
      <h2>How to opt out</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Block or delete cookies for auxsays.com, or block third-party scripts, in your browser's privacy settings or with a content blocker.</li>
        <li>Install the <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout">Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on</a> that Google provides.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>The site works the same either way. Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites</a>.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="methodology-card">
      <h2>Kept only in your browser</h2>
      <p>The products you watch and the versions you mark as installed in My Patch Stack are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to AUXSAYS or to Google. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="methodology-card">
      <h2>Other requests</h2>
      <p>The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Most pages load an animation library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com; the error page and the article category and tag pages load fonts and styles from Google Fonts and jsDelivr instead. Like any website, these services receive the usual information your browser sends with a request, including your IP address.</p>
    </article>
