  Hub 1.9.1 by clicking on the link below. Windows Camera Hub 1.9.1.msi macOS Camera Hub 1.9.1.pkg What’s new in Camera Hub
  1.9.1? This release is chock-full of new features and improvements to your camera and Prompter experience. Note: After Camera
  Hub is updated, your Prompter’s firmware will automatically update. This will result in Prompter turning off momentarily.
  Your multi-monitor configuration may shift and then re-appear as normal once the firmware update has been completed. 🆕 Introducing
  Facecam MK.2 Our iconic webcam gets even better. The latest model offers everything you love about Facecam, plus new features
  like HDR, a built-in privacy shutter, and pan-tilt-zoom effects. With improved image quality, Facecam MK.2 ensures streamers
  and professionals look as true-to-life as ever—even in dim lighting. Its sleek design fits naturally in modern workspaces,
  while a lower profile enhances eye contact. Starting today, Facecam MK.2 is available to order online, with availability
  at major retailers around the world coming soon. Learn more about Facecam MK.2 🆕 Facecam Pro firmware update A new firmware
  is available for Facecam Pro, making the world’s first 4K60 webcam even better: Say hello to tint control. You can now fine-tune
  your magenta-green tint settings, alongside your blue-orange white balance. This makes it easy to lock in the perfect color
  balance, just like a professional camera. You can now lock in your auto white balance setting at any time. Saving settings
  directly to your webcam is more reliable than ever. Camera Hub is better able to detect when your camera is being used in
  other software, such as OBS Studio. Learn how to update your Facecam Pro firmware. 🎥 Face Tracking* Leveraging NVIDIA AI
  technology, Camera Hub can now follow your face as it moves. This ensures you’re always centered in frame, saving you time
  and focus. *To use face tracking, you’ll need an NVIDIA RTX 2060 GPU or better. What''s new with Prompter? Alright, let''s
  look at Prompter changes. All of these have been highly requested by you, our community. Starting us off is text mode. ⬇️
  Save Multiple scripts You can now save multiple scripts to Camera Hub and switch between them on the fly. Drag and drop
  to reorder your scripts, or rename scripts with a simple right click. 🗒️ Automatic Chapters No more manually setting up
  script chapters one by one. Now when you paste text into Camera Hub, chapters will automatically form at every blank line.
  👇 Chapter Drag-n-drop Moving around chapters is now as simple as drag and drop. Restructuring your scripts has never been
  easier or faster. 🪓 Easily Split Chapters Split long chapters into two by right clicking or pressing SHIFT + ENTER. This
  helps keep your text readable and organized. 🔁 Looping Script Do as many takes as you need. Prompter will automatically
  loop your script, so you can stay in frame without fussing over controls. 🎙️ Voice Dictation Now you can enter a script
  using only your voice. Just select the microphone icon to bring up your system’s built-in voice-to-text transcription. Great
  for recording podcasts, taking meeting notes, and making your script sound more natural. 🗨️ Resize text in the editor Now
  you can resize text in Camera Hub’s editor, separate from the text displayed on Prompter. 🎯 Crosshair overlay Thanks to
  a suggestion from Harris Heller , Prompter can now display a crosshair overlay to pinpoint your camera lens and improve
  eye contact even further. Choose from a selection of crosshair styles or add your own, no matter which mode you’re in—Display,
  Text, or Chat. (Oh and let us know if you spot the Easter egg.) Learn more about configuring a crosshair. 🔘 Turn Prompter
  On/Off Want to turn off Prompter’s display when not in use? Now you can. ☀️ Adjustable contrast* Adjust the contrast of
  Prompter’s screen* to make colors more or less vibrant. *Only available on Windows currently 👀 OpenDyslexic font With the
  addition of OpenDyslexic , a dyslexia-friendly font, Prompter is now more accessible. (Special thanks to your feedback during
  the recent Camera Hub beta.) 🗣️ Twitch Chat improvements You can now right-click a channel in Camera Hub to open Twitch
  in your browser When you enter a Twitch channel name, Camera Hub will warn you if no such channel exists 🛠️ Bug fixes and
  performance improvements When you create a script, Camera Hub will automatically enter placeholder text in a new chapter
  Prompter fonts are now sorted alphabetically You can now select your firmware, serial, and software info to copy version
  numbers When you receive a firmware update prompt, Camera Hub will automatically switch to the Camera tab We updated the
  Windows MJPEG decoder to improve overall performance The Taskbar icon now adapts to system-wide dark and light mode settings
  In the Effects tab, you can now use special characters when entering file paths for background images and videos In rare
  cases, applications on macOS could no longer be controlled with the mouse when on Prompter Face Tracking is only shown when
  a capable GPU is detected. Face Tracking now works correctly when the Mirror/Flip video effect is used The Camera Hub Stream
  Deck plugin now supports Facecam MK.2, tint control, and many other features introduced with Camera Hub 1.9.'
