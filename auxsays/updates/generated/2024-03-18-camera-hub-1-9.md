official_patch_notes_body: 'Release Date: 18 March 2024 What’s new in Camera Hub 1.9? This release is chock-full of new features
  and improvements to your camera and Prompter experience. Note: After Camera Hub is updated, your Prompter’s firmware will
  automatically update. This will result in Prompter turning off momentarily. Your multi-monitor configuration may shift and
  then re-appear as normal once the firmware update has been completed. Windows Camera Hub 1.9.msi macOS Camera Hub 1.9.pkg
  🆕 Facecam Pro firmware update A new firmware is available for Facecam Pro, making your camera even better. Fully manual
  tint control - almost unheard of in webcams, you can now control tint (green/purple) alongside white balance (blue/orange).
  Locking in the perfect color has never been easier. Auto white balance can now be locked If you like the current setting.
  Saving settings to flash memory has been made more reliable. Improved detection of when the camera is in use. Learn how
  to update your Facecam Pro firmware. 🎥 Face Tracking Camera Hub can now keep you centered in the frame when moving around.
  This uses the same underlying NVIDIA-based* technologies as Eye Contact . *Face Tracking requires an NVIDIA RTX 2060 GPU
  or better. Alright, let''s look at Prompter changes. All of these have been highly requested by you, our community. Starting
  us off is text mode. ⬇️ Save Multiple scripts You can now save multiple scripts and quickly switch between them. You can
  also drag-and-drop to reorder your script and right-click and select rename to change the script''s title. 🗒️ Automatic
  Chapters When pasting text into a script chapters are now automatically created at blank lines. 👇 Chapter Drag-n-drop Re-arranging
  chapters is now as simple as drag and drop. 🪓 Easily Split Chapters Chapters can now be split by pressing SHIFT+ENTER or
  using the context menu. 🔁 Looping Script Take 1. Take 2. Take 3. It''s now easier thanks to the new script loop feature
  that will continuously scroll your script. 🎙️ Voice Dictation Click the microphone icon to bring up Windows or Mac''s built-in
  voice-to-text transcription. This can come in handy when recording podcasts, taking notes during a meeting, or recording
  a script to sound like spoken word and then editing it later. 🗨️ Resize text in the editor We heard your feedback, and the
  text size in the editor can now be adjusted separately from the text on Prompter. 🎯 Crosshair overlay Credit to Harris Heller
  for this one. You can now turn on a crosshair across all three modes - Display, Text, and Chat- which will help you pinpoint
  where the camera is and where your eye focus should be. This is especially helpful when reading scripts. Oh, and we left
  an Easter egg in there. Let us know if you find it. We’ve included some default pointer styles, and you can even add your
  own. Learn more about configuring a crosshair. 🔘 Turn Prompter On/Off You can now turn the Prompters display off when not
  in use. ☀️ Adjustable contrast* Prompters contrast can now be adjusted. Contrast affects the intensity of colors and can
  make Prompter appear more vibrant. *Contrast adjustment only available on Windows at the moment. 👀 OpenDyslexic font Based
  on feedback during the Camera Hub beta from you, our community, we added the dyslexia-friendly font OpenDyslexic to improve
  accessibility. 🗣️ Twitch Chat improvements Easily navigate directly to the selected Twitch channel by right-clicking and
  selecting open in browser. Improvements have been made to ensure the channel you enter, you know, exists in case of typos.
  🛠️ Bug fixes and performance improvements When a new script is created, an empty chapter with placeholder text is automatically
  started. Fonts for Prompter are now sorted alphabetically. Firmware, serial, and software version numbers can be copied
  simply by clicking on them. Camera Hub automatically switches to the "Camera" tab when the prompt for an available firmware
  update is displayed Updated the MJPEG decoder on Windows to improve overall performance. The taskbar icon now adapts to
  system-wide dark and light mode settings. Special characters are handled for file paths of background images/videos in the
  effects tab. The Stream Deck plugin for Camera Hub has been updated to support tint control with Facecam Pro and many of
  the Prompter features mentioned above. Don''t have it yet? Grab it from Marketplace.'
