  an improved interface overall. Wave Link 3 is free, and will also work with a wide variety of microphones, not just those
  from Elgato. Wave Link 2 has reached End of Life. It will still be available for download, but our development focus will
  be on Wave Link 3 moving forward. We look forward to improve audio for an increasing audience of great creators. Release
  Date : 21 April 2026 💻 Download Links Windows Elgato Wave Link 3.1.msix (x64) Elgato Wave Link 3.1.msix (Arm64) What''s
  new in Wave Link 3.1? 🎛️ More control over your audio effects You can now rename, copy, paste, and reorder your audio effects
  across channels and devices. Give each effect a name that makes sense to you, and your custom names will carry through to
  the Wave Link Stream Deck plugin so everything stays organized exactly how you set it up. 🔇 Hide audio devices you don''t
  need Monitors, webcams, and peripherals often bring extra audio inputs and outputs along for the ride. You can deactivate
  those unused devices directly within the app, removing them from your system-wide audio settings without digging through
  Windows Sound settings. 🔌 Inline Boost Compatibility Using a Cloudlifter or FetHead in your signal chain? A new Inline Boost
  Compatibility toggle keeps the XLR input active and prevents the auto-mute behavior that can occur when an inline booster
  is detected. Find it in your device settings. 🔗 Smarter app routing The "Add app to channel" dropdown now organizes apps
  into categories, making it easier to find what you''re looking for. 🖐️ Improved drag-and-drop Channel and mix reordering
  now has a clearer visual drag handle, making it easier to rearrange your layout. ✅ VST Audio Effect Plugin scan overhaul
  Plugin scans now show a progress indicator so you always know what''s happening in the background. Broken plugins are handled
  gracefully without repeated crashes, and you''ll be notified which plugins caused issues when the scan completes. Wave Link
  also warns you if your configured VST2 directory path looks non-standard before scanning begins. 🛑 Wave Link updates won''t
  reset your audio apps Wave Link no longer reinstalls the driver on every update — so apps like OBS Studio and Voicemeeter
  keep their audio device configuration intact. When an update does include a driver change, we''ll give you a heads up. 🛠️
  Bug fixes and performance improvements Last viewed tab - Wave Link will now remember if you were on the Mixer or Device
  view and open to that tab. Device name tooltips - Long device names in the sidebar now truncate with an ellipsis, and hovering
  over them reveals the full name. Muted mix indicator - When an entire virtual mix is muted, the mix header now shows a visual
  indicator so it''s always clear at a glance. Double monitoring warning restored - The warning that appears when a Wave device
  is added to a currently monitored mix and the Mic/PC ratio is adjusted has been restored. Better sleep/wake recovery - Wave
  Link recovers more reliably after your system wakes from sleep. Wave Link は日本語に対応しました - If your system language is set to
  Japanese, Wave Link will automatically appear in your language. Fixed a crash that could occur when a second Wave Link instance
  was launched before the first had fully started. Fixed Wave Link failing to start when installed on a drive other than C.
  Fixed the "Driver Issue" screen appearing in a loop even after running Repair Driver. Fixed the Wave Link window opening
  behind other apps when launched from the system tray after a silent start. Fixed output channel effects (Expander, Compressor,
  and Sound Check) not saving their on/off state between sessions. Fixed a crash that could occur when manually scanning VSTs
  in certain scenarios. Fixed the output volume slider continuously drifting instead of staying at the set value. Fixed the
  VST include/exclude direct monitor toggle for self-monitoring not working. Fixed output volume resetting to 100% after restart
  on ARM64 devices. Fixed firmware updates not starting on ARM64 devices.'
