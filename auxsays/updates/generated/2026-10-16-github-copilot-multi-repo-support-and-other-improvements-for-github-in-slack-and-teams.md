  \n Review progress: “@GitHub Summarize recent activity across our repositories.”\n\n\n You can ask these questions directly\
  \ in Slack or Microsoft Teams, making it easier to stay on top of work across projects without leaving the conversation.\n\
  \n\n Additional improvements\n We’ve also made several improvements to GitHub Copilot in Slack and Microsoft Teams:\n\n\n\
  \ More natural conversations: Mention @GitHub anywhere in your message, not just at the beginning.\n\n Smoother repository\
  \ switching: Move between repositories more easily during a conversation.\n\n Better Slack conversation context: @GitHub\
