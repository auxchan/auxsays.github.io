# Relationship model

The current persistent world contains no accepted or factual structural edges. Its 1,110 hierarchy tethers mean only “organized under / drill into.” Its 2,000 synthetic influences exist solely to stress the renderer. Both are `TEST_FIXTURE`, `SYNTHETIC`, and `NEVER_ACCEPTED_NEVER_PUBLISHED`.

An accepted relationship must have stable identities, version, direction, source evidence, coefficient/weight semantics, units, valid/knowledge time, lag/buffer/substitution/capacity rules where applicable, provenance, rights posture, and acceptance status. Propagation may traverse only eligible accepted edges.

Phase-4B’s remaining hard boundary is the authoritative industry-to-commodity handoff. Direct BEA cells may become candidates, but total-requirements tables must not be recursively traversed or double-counted. Common-cause reconciliation and bounded termination are required. Until accepted edges exist, glowing graph lines must never be described as economic impact.
